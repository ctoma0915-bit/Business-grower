FAQ

Questions you might have

Something else on your mind? Email me at ctoma0915@gmail.com and I'll give you a straight answer.

Who do you work with?

Local service businesses where one new customer is worth a lot: roofing, HVAC, plumbing, solar, med spas, law firms, real estate and insurance.

How much does a lead cost?

It depends on your trade, your area and what a customer is worth to you. I quote a fixed price per lead before we start, and you only pay it for leads that qualify. The calculator above shows what a lead is worth to you, so you can judge any price I quote.

What counts as a qualified lead?

A real person inside your service area who asked for the service you sell and gave a working phone number or email. We write this definition down before we start. Duplicates, spam and wrong-area calls don't count.

How do I know the leads are real?

Every lead comes through a tracked phone number or form, and you can see the source of each one.

Is there a contract?

A simple written agreement that defines a lead, the price and how we track it. No long lock-in.

What if the leads aren't good?

Tell me. Leads that don't match our written definition aren't billed. If the test doesn't work for you, you stop, and you've only paid for leads that qualified.

What do you need from me?

Your service area, the jobs you want more of, and someone to answer the phone. Leads go cold fast, so the quicker you call back, the more jobs you close.
