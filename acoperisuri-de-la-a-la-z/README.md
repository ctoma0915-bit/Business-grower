# Acoperișuri de la A la Z

Texts for the Facebook page, Instagram account and WhatsApp Business profile of a roofing firm.

**The idea behind the texts:** the name promises that the firm handles everything about a roof, from A to Z. Every text repeats that promise: you send a few photos, and the firm handles the rest.

**Before publishing:**
- Area: București și Ilfov (confirmed by Toma, 13 Nov 2026).
- Check that 0729 373 800 is the number customers should use.
- Remove any service he doesn't offer.

## Brand

Files are in `brand/`:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `profil-1080.png` | Profile picture on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Business (1080×1080, safe for the round crop) |
| `coperta-facebook-1640x624.png` | Facebook cover photo |
| `logo.png` | Main logo, dark text, transparent background |
| `logo-alb.png` | Logo with white text, transparent background, for dark photos and backgrounds |
| `simbol.svg` / `simbol-fundal-inchis.svg` | The symbol alone, as a vector, for light and dark backgrounds |

**The symbol:**
- The **A** is drawn as a roof.
- One **copper** line forms the A's crossbar and runs on into the **Z**: "de la A la Z" in a single stroke.
- Copper also hints at copper gutters.

**Colours**
- Slate `#2B3440`
- Copper: `#B8692A` on light backgrounds, `#E08A45` on dark ones
- White

**Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (Google Fonts).
- "ACOPERIȘURI": ExtraBold 800.
- "DE LA A LA Z": SemiBold 600, spread to the same width as the word above, with the Z in copper.

## First 3 posts (before any ads, so the page doesn't look empty)

Use only his real photos and jobs. Don't invent numbers, years or guarantees.

1. **Introduction:** a photo of his team or of a finished roof, plus:
   ```
   Acoperișuri de la A la Z: ne ocupăm de tot ce ține de acoperișul casei, de la prima evaluare până la ultimul detaliu. Montaj, reparații, jgheaburi, mansarde. Sunați-ne la 0729 373 800 și vă spunem ce e de făcut.
   ```
2. **"De la A la Z" explained:** a photo of a roof in progress, plus:
   ```
   Ce înseamnă „de la A la Z”?
   A – Analizăm acoperișul și vă spunem sincer ce e de făcut
   B – Alegem împreună materialele potrivite
   …
   Z – Zero griji: noi ne ocupăm de tot, dumneavoastră doar alegeți
   Sunați-ne pentru o ofertă: 0729 373 800
   ```
3. **A finished job:** before and after photos, plus:
   ```
   Acoperiș refăcut în [localitate]. [Material] și jgheaburi noi. De la A la Z, fără grija clientului.
   ```
