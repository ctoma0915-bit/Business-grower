## 0. Before starting

- **Client's OK:** the messages speak in the client's name. Get the client's OK on the texts, and on what they promise (free visit, area, type of work).
- **Who we contact:** businesses only. Use the phone, email or contact form that a business publishes for itself.
- **Who we don't contact:** private people (homeowners) by phone, SMS, WhatsApp or email, unless they reached out first. Collecting their numbers and messaging them without consent breaks the GDPR rules on unsolicited marketing. Homeowners are reached through the channels in section 3 instead.
- **Opting out:** if anyone says "no" or "don't write again", note it in the tracker and never contact them again.

## 3. Homeowners: channels that are allowed

Homeowners can't be messaged cold. These channels let them come to us:

- **Neighborhood Facebook groups** (`Locuitorii din Voluntari`, `Bragadiru – anunțuri`, `Pipera`, `Otopeni`, `Chiajna`, `Popești-Leordeni` and similar). Post a short text with 2–3 job photos, if the group allows ads. Reply only to people who comment or write first.
- **Flyers in house neighborhoods** in Ilfov and on the outskirts of București (fluturași in mailboxes): photo, "Infiltrații? Acoperiș vechi?", a WhatsApp number and a QR code to the landing page.
- **Google reviews:** ask happy clients for reviews. People who search Google Maps for a roofer call directly.
- **Referrals:** after every finished job, ask "Mai știți pe cineva cu probleme la acoperiș?" ("Do you know anyone else with roof problems?").

Homeowners who write or comment first can be contacted, by the same rule as the form leads.
