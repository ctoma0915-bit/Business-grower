# Acoperișuri România

Brand kit and page texts for a roofing firm that works in every region of Romania.

"Acoperișuri România" is a working name. It changes if he picks another one.

## Brand

Files are in `brand/`:

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `profil-1080.png` | Profile picture on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Business (1080×1080, safe for the round crop) |
| `coperta-facebook-1640x624.png` | Facebook cover photo |
| `logo.png` | Main logo, dark text, transparent background (offers, documents, light ads) |
| `logo-alb.png` | Logo with white text, transparent background (dark photos and backgrounds) |
| `simbol.svg` | The symbol alone, as a vector |

**The symbol:** a roof over a house in the colours of the flag, meaning roofs all over Romania.

**Colours**
- Charcoal `#1C1F26`
- Flag colours: blue `#002B7F`, yellow `#FCD116`, red `#CE1126`
- White

Use the flag colours only in the symbol and in thin bars, never for large backgrounds or for text.

**Font:** Archivo (Google Fonts).
- "ACOPERIȘURI": ExtraBold 800.
- "ROMÂNIA": SemiBold 600, spread to the same width as the word above.

### First 3 posts (before any ads, so the page doesn't look empty)

Use only his real photos and jobs. Don't invent numbers, years or guarantees.

1. **Introduction:** the cover photo plus: `Montăm și reparăm acoperișuri în toată România. Trimiteți-ne poze cu acoperișul pe WhatsApp și vă spunem ce e de făcut.`
2. **A finished job:** before and after photos plus: `Acoperiș refăcut în [localitate, județ]. [Material] și jgheaburi noi.`
3. **Prepare for winter:** a photo of a roof or gutters plus: `Înainte de iarnă, verificați țiglele/tabla, jgheaburile și coama. Dacă vedeți pete pe tavan sau infiltrații, trimiteți-ne poze.`
