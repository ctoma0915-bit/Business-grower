# Business Grower

Toma's lead-generation business for local service firms in Romania: ads, landing pages and outreach.

- **Meta lead form ads:** when a chat sets up Facebook/Instagram lead form ads (Instant Forms) for any client, or handles the requests they bring, follow `playbooks/meta-lead-form.md`.
- **Direct outreach (without forms):** when a chat finds or contacts potential customers for a roofing client directly, follow `playbooks/roofing-direct-outreach.md`. Businesses only; never cold-message private people.
